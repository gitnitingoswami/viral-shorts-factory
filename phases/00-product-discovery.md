# Phase 00 — Product Discovery, PRD and Architecture

Act as CTO, product architect, and technical analyst. This phase is planning and research only.

## Objective
Define a feasible, secure, testable product for an AI-assisted short-form video content business.

## Required work
1. Inspect the workspace and identify existing code, conventions, and constraints.
2. Define target users, primary workflows, MVP scope, non-goals, assumptions, and success metrics.
3. Design the end-to-end flow: niche selection → trend research → ideas → scripts → storyboard → assets/voice → rendering → review → publishing → analytics → finance.
4. Compare technical options and explain trade-offs.
5. Define Angular, NestJS, PostgreSQL/TypeORM, Redis/BullMQ, FFmpeg, storage, AI provider abstractions, and deployment architecture.
6. Identify external services and verify current official docs, API availability, terms, quotas, pricing, OAuth scopes, and regional/account restrictions. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
7. Define database domains and a first-pass ERD.
8. Define threat model, data privacy, account access, approval gates, audit logs, and secret management.
9. Estimate operating costs with ranges and explicit assumptions. Do not promise views, virality, revenue, or monetization.
10. Propose phased delivery plan and acceptance criteria.
