# Task intake

1. Identify whether the request belongs to the Viral Shorts Factory roadmap or a separate project.
2. Inspect the actual repository before assuming frameworks, modules, or conventions.
3. Summarize expected business outcome, current behavior, acceptance criteria, assumptions, dependencies, and risks.
4. Identify impacted modules, API/database changes, security concerns, and test strategy.
5. For Viral Shorts Factory work, check `docs/PHASE_TRACKER.md` and follow the earliest eligible phase.
6. For general engineering work, assign bounded tasks to the relevant specialist agents and let the Lead Orchestrator coordinate.
7. After the initial project authorization, execute autonomously within `CLAUDE.md` and `APPROVAL_POLICY.md`.
8. Validate with real test/build/lint/typecheck commands and report exact outcomes.
9. Record changes, blockers, and delivery summary. Do not fabricate test results or external-action success.
